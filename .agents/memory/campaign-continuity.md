---
name: Campaign continuity
description: Progress continuity when the original short campaign was expanded and its environments moved into difficulty bands.
---

Preserve earned numeric completion records when changing campaign structure, rather than resetting saves or moving completion records to new environmental positions.

**Why:** The campaign expanded from fifteen to exactly ninety-nine levels and moved the original special environments into later difficulty bands. Keeping numeric progress lets existing players continue without erasing earned results or automatically skipping the new difficulty progression.

**How to apply:** Future campaign changes should extend existing saves. A player who completed the former final level can enter the next numbered level; environmental similarity alone does not justify marking a later level complete.
